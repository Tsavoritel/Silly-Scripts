Project Documentation
The goal of the game is to feed a village that is short on food by stealing it from greedy monsters. The idea is to find a balance in keeping the village fed.

My largest operating pricipal was to not hard-code anything if possible, or in other words, make everything either an object or variable that could be changed. This made programming the project at the beginning somewhat tedious, but after I had the fundamentals complete it made later development significantly easier.

How is the game related to sustainability?
Going back to the main idea, the player has the option to continue even when the village is fed. Obviously this is unnecessary, and there are story consequencies of doing so. The idea here is to help the player understand how to balance consumption.
